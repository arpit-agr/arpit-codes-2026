Hi, I'm a web designer-developer based in Mumbai, India. I focus on semantic HTML, modern CSS and accessibility to create interfaces that work everywhere, including in your framework of choice.

About Me

In 2019, I watched my cousin build a website from scratch. Seeing it take shape in real time felt pretty magical. That was it for me. I left chartered accountancy and taught myself to build for the web.

Since then, I've designed and built websites for clients from the ground up, handling everything from prototyping in the browser to development and deployment. I write CSS that adapts to its context without relying on hardcoded breakpoints or magic numbers. I support every browser and optimise for none, and I test with keyboard navigation and screen readers to make sure what I build actually works for everyone.

I want to work alongside developers who care about the end-user experience — building websites that are inclusive and accessible regardless of someone's ability, device, or connection speed. There's always more to learn, and that's the part I look forward to most.
