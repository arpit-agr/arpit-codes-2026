About Me

In 2019, I was studying chartered accountancy when I watched my cousin design and build a website from scratch. It was magical. I knew I'd found the work I wanted to do. So I taught myself to build for the web.

Since then, I've designed and built websites for clients from the ground up, handling everything from prototyping in the browser to development and deployment. I write CSS that communicates my design intent to the browser, relying as little as possible on hardcoded breakpoints or magic numbers. I support every browser and optimise for none, and I test to make sure what I build is accessible.

I want to work alongside developers who care about the end-user experience, about building websites that are inclusive.
